| `load_earthquakes` | U.S. Geological Survey, Earthquake Hazards Program, real-time feed `4.5_month` (https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/), snapshot generated 2026-10-30 13:33 UTC | Public domain (U.S. Government work) |
